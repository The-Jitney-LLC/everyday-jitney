Store access and browsing analytics

The store entry screen combines acceptance of the enterprise terms with this analytics notice. Choosing “Agree & enter” after checking the acknowledgment also permits the analytics described here. Visiting a page alone does not grant that permission.

The Jitney LLC uses ICP analytics to measure page and product views, visible browsing time, scroll depth, image clicks, variant choices, cart activity and checkout starts. Records include a random session identifier, timestamps, page path, viewport size, referring origin and campaign tags. Events are sent to the company’s Make.com analytics workflow. This beacon does not record typed form contents or card details.

A first-party session identifier uses a rolling 30-minute cookie. This is pseudonymous activity data, not a guarantee of anonymity. Analytics is disabled before acceptance, when you choose “Accept terms without analytics,” or when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.
